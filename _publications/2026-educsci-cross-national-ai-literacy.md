Guided by Social Cognitive Theory and the Technology Acceptance Model, this qualitative study examines how K–12 teachers across five countries — the United States (n = 7), India (n = 5), Qatar (n = 5), Colombia (n = 5), and the Philippines (n = 4) — conceptualize AI literacy and integrate generative AI into their practice. Through 26 semi-structured interviews conducted in summer and fall 2025, three cross-national patterns were identified that challenge dominant narratives about AI adoption in education: (1) institutional support did not uniformly predict AI literacy depth; (2) prompt engineering awareness functioned as a critical differentiator between pedagogical engagement and opaque productivity tool use; and (3) AI use for lesson preparation far outpaced classroom-facing application across all contexts.
